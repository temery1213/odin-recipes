Ribeye

Home Grilled Cheese Ribeye Mac-n-Cheese

Ingredients

  • Ribeye
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter
  • Rosemary
  • Olive oil

Preparation

  1. Heat cast iron pan on high
  2. Rub steak with salt, pepper, and any other seasonings you may want
  3. Place steak on to pan
  4. Add a dollop of butter, continue spooning the butter onto the steak
  5. Add rosemary to pan
  6. Cook for 1 minute or longer depending on desired finished temperature
  7. Once both sides are seared, grab with tongs and sear the edges
  8. Serve
