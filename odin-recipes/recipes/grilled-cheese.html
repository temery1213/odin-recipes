Grilled Cheese

Home Grilled Cheese Ribeye Mac-n-Cheese

Ingredients

  1. Bread
  2. Cheese
  3. Butter

Preparation

  • Spread butter on to each slice of bread
  • Place both slices of bread on pan butter side down
  • Place a slice of cheese on each slice of bread
  • Cook for one minute, then combine the slices and cook until golden on both sides
